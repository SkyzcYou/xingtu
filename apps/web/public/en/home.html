Questions?

What is Xingtu?

Xingtu is a production-grade AI creation platform for creators and enterprise teams, built to organize image generation, prompt reasoning, flowcharts, mind maps, asset libraries, and creative work.

How is it different from regular AI chat?

AI chat tools are great for conversation. Xingtu is designed for execution: generated results, references, notes, and project structure stay together on one canvas.

What production workflows can Xingtu support?

Use it for covers, posters, product visuals, storyboards, process planning, knowledge organization, multi-version exploration, and delivery reviews.

What does “Creativity + AI + Canvas” mean?

Creativity is the starting point, AI provides image, video, and text generation, and the canvas preserves context, assets, and structure so teams can build reusable creative productivity.
